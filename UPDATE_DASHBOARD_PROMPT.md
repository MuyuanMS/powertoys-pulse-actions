dispatch and publication. PR workers use only powertoys-pr-review's worker
contract and the reference for their current operation. Issue judgments and

incrementally. Do not convert a normal tick into an uncapped drain.

Publish to canonical actions main under existing authorization and verify
Pulse synchronization through the approved path. Notifications/project sync
must not stop otherwise runnable review or artifact work.

Report delivered/current/author-wait/pending/deferred PRs, issue judgments and
designs, invalid issue artifacts corrected/remaining, canonical commit and
Pulse delivery. No upstream writes without separate explicit approval.
